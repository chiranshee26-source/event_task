## Cybersecurity news I recently read about

**EY third-party platform breach (July 2026)**

Ernst & Young disclosed a data breach involving a third-party IT service management platform used by employees doing tax work for clients. EY first detected unusual activity in April 2026, and the exposed data reportedly included client tax records and Social Security numbers.

**What I took from it:** Even big companies with strong security can be exposed through the vendors they rely on. Third-party risk matters as much as your own defenses, so it's worth vetting vendors, limiting the data they can access, and monitoring for unusual activity.
